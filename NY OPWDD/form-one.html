CANS assessment appointment request

Complete this form to request a Child and Adolescent Needs and Strengths (CANS) assessment appointment for children aged 17 or younger. You will get an email receipt from Maximus within 1 business day of this request. Your information is secure and will only be used to schedule your CANS assessment.

Disclaimer: Completion of this form does not guarantee or confirm an appointment.

Pre-submission verification: Before submitting a request, confirm that the person is currently assigned for assessment with Maximus by following up with the Care Manager, CCO/DDRO or through the OPWDD Choices system. An appointment will not be scheduled for a person not assigned to Maximus.

Next steps

Step 1 - Request received

The Maximus CANS scheduling team will reply within 1 business day to tell you we got your request.

Step 2 - Appointment confirmation

A CANS scheduler will confirm your appointment by email within 3 business days. Or they will request more information, if needed.

Step 3 - Confirmation email

Once scheduled, you and your care manager will get a confirmation email from Maximus with the appointment details.
