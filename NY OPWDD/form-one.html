What happens next?

Step 1 - Request received

The Maximus CANS scheduling team will reply within 24 hours to acknowledge receipt of your request.

Step 2 - Appointment confirmation

A CANS scheduler will confirm your appointment by email within three business days or request additional information, if needed.

Step 3 - Confirmation email

Once scheduled, you and your care manager will receive a confirmation email from Maximus with the appointment details.

Important information

If the individual has not been assigned to Maximus for a CANS assessment, the scheduling coordinator will let you know in the email response.
