CAS assessment appointment request

Complete this form to request a Coordinated Assessment System (CAS) assessment for adults aged 18 or older. You will get an email receipt from Maximus within 1 business day of this request. Your information is secure and will only be used to schedule your CAS assessment.

Disclaimer: Completion of this form does not guarantee or confirm an appointment.

Pre-submission verification: Before submitting a request, confirm that the person is currently assigned for assessment with Maximus by following up with the Care Manager, CCO/DDRO or through the OPWDD Choices system. An appointment will not be scheduled for a person not assigned to Maximus.

Fields marked with an asterisk (*) are required.

1. Requestor information

2. Person who needs the assessment

3. Care management information

4. Is an interpreter required?*

5. Appointment preferences

You must select at least two days. Your preferred day and time are requests. We will schedule your appointment based on assessor's availability.

6. How would you like to complete the CAS assessment?*

Telehealth requirements: laptop/computer, smartphone, or tablet with camera.

Next steps

Step 1 - Request received

The Maximus CAS scheduling team will reply within 1 business day to tell you we got your request.

Step 2 - Appointment confirmation

A CAS scheduler will confirm your appointment by email within 3 business days. Or they will request more information, if needed.

Step 3 - Confirmation email

Once scheduled, you and your care manager will get a confirmation email from Maximus with the appointment details.

Important information

If the person has not been assigned to Maximus for a CAS assessment, we will tell you in an email.
